FAQ
Common questions from fund managers answered.
Do I need to launch a new fund to use Tokeniser?
No. Tokeniser can support new funds, feeder structures, staged digital versions of an existing fund, and migrations of existing funds.
Do I need to replace my fund administrator?
No. Tokeniser works alongside your existing administrators, trustees, custodians and other service providers.
Does tokenisation guarantee liquidity?
No. Tokeniser can enable secondary transfers and connectivity to liquidity and financing infrastructure. Actual market liquidity depends on the fund, the underlying assets and investor demand.
Can Tokeniser work with our existing investor portal?
Yes. Managers can use Tokeniser's interfaces or integrate their existing investor experience through APIs.
Do investors need to understand the underlying technology?
The digital infrastructure can be abstracted from the investor experience. The level of direct digital ownership will depend on the operating model the fund chooses.
Can fund units be used as collateral?
Suitable assets can be configured to support collateral, borrowing and other protocol finance capabilities.
