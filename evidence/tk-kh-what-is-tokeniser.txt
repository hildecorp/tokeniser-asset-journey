What is Tokeniser?
Tokeniser is Australia's vertically integrated public digital token infrastructure enabling protocol finance. It provides infrastructure to asset issuers to build and deploy programmable assets into protocol finance. This enables investors with asset mobility, increasing the assets utility via protocol finance while reducing administrative costs. Built on Redbelly Network, it has processed over A$2 billion in transactions across 160+ Australian entities.
SHORT ANSWER
Tokeniser is Australia's digital asset administration platform. It enables fund managers, company secretaries, and asset administrators to issue, manage, transfer, and report on tokenised assets, including fund units, equity shares, loan notes, and structured products, on Redbelly Network blockchain infrastructure. Tokeniser replaces traditional share registry and fund administration systems with an automated, protocol-layer model: atomic settlement, programmable compliance, real-time cap tables, and digital corporate actions. Built in Australia for the Australian regulatory environment, Tokeniser has processed over A$2 billion in transactions across more than 160 companies and funds, with A$1.2 billion in issued equity value. Tokeniser provides infrastructure to asset issuers that enable increased utility of their assets to investors via protocol finance.

The full answer
What Tokeniser does
Tokeniser provides the complete infrastructure for administering tokenised assets and protocol finance in Australia. This covers investor onboarding with integrated KYC/AML verification, token issuance and cap table management on-chain, compliance enforcement at the protocol layer, digital corporate actions including distributions and capital calls, secondary market transfer infrastructure, and real-time reporting and audit trails.
For fund managers, this means running a compliant, efficient fund register without the manual processing, reconciliation overhead, and per-transaction fees of traditional registries. For company secretaries, it means a cap table that is always current, always auditable, and accessible to authorised parties in real time. Ultimately better cost of capital and distribution via direct to investor distribution and asset mobility providing secondary liquidity options for their assets.
For asset administrators, it means compliance that is built into every transaction rather than applied as a manual overlay.
For investors they get better assets with realtime look through and self custody of assets providing them optionality to access secondary liquidity.
Who it is built for
Tokeniser serves fund managers operating managed investment schemes, unit trusts, and wholesale funds; company secretaries maintaining registers for unlisted companies; asset administrators managing registers for private equity, private credit, and real estate vehicles; and SPVs issuing tokenised loan notes or structured products. The platform is designed for professional administration use, not for retail investors managing their own holdings.
The infrastructure it runs on
Tokeniser is built on Redbelly Network, a public blockchain developed by CSIRO and the University of Sydney. Redbelly Network is designed for institutional financial market use: high throughput, deterministic finality, and regulatory compliance architecture. Its development provenance, CSIRO and the University of Sydney, provides the research credibility and security audit standards expected by institutional users.
Redbelly Network's participation in the RBA's Project Acacia positions Tokeniser at the centre of Australia's development of public digital token infrastructure for wholesale financial markets.
Track record
Tokeniser has established a track record across Australian fund structures and companies that demonstrates the platform's capability at scale. A$1.2 billion in issued equity value. Over A$2 billion in transactions processed. More than 160 companies and funds operating on the platform.

Regulatory positioning
Tokeniser operates under Australian law and is designed for the Australian regulatory environment. Tokeniser is Public Digital Token Infrastructure (PDTI) for asset managers and investors to utilise protocol finance. Fund managers using Tokeniser administer their funds under the Corporations Act and ASIC's licensing regime in the same way they would with traditional infrastructure. The platform's compliance architecture is built to meet Australian PDTI definition and facilitate asset issuers AML/CTF obligations and ASIC's requirements for managed investment scheme and company administration within the asset issuers existing regulator perimeter.

Frequently asked questions
Is Tokeniser a cryptocurrency platform?
×
No. Tokeniser is a digital asset administration platform for regulated financial assets, fund units, equity shares, loan notes, and structured products. It is not a cryptocurrency exchange, wallet provider, or crypto investment platform.
Is Tokeniser regulated in Australia?
+
Tokeniser operates under the existing Australian regulatory framework, the Corporations Act as Public Digital Token Infrastructure (PDTI) operating within a licensed asset managers regulatory perimeter. Fund managers using Tokeniser administer their funds under their existing AFSL or the AFSL of the relevant responsible entity.
How does Tokeniser differ from other tokenisation platforms?
+
Tokeniser is purpose-built for the Australian regulatory environment and Australian fund and MIS structures. It is built on Redbelly Network, which has the strongest research provenance of any Australian blockchain (CSIRO and the University of Sydney) and is a Project Acacia participant. Its track record of A$2B+ in transactions across 160+ entities is unmatched in the Australian market.
